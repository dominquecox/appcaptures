Support

Get AppCaptures help, make privacy requests, or remove your waitlist email.

For help with AppCaptures or its waitlist, email support@appcaptures.com. Include what happened and the browser you used. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents.

Frequently asked questions

What will the waitlist emails cover?

Launch and major-template updates. Joining is free, and you can unsubscribe or remove your email at any time.

Does the signup message confirm a server entry?

The page can save a local copy if the connection fails, so the message alone does not guarantee your email reached us. Email support if you need help checking your signup.

Do I need an account to request help?

No. The website’s waitlist does not create an account.

Privacy requests

To request access, correction, or a copy of your information, email support@appcaptures.com from the address concerned and describe your request. See our Privacy Policy for details. For deletion, follow the removal steps below.

Remove your email / unsubscribe

  1. Email support@appcaptures.com from the address you used to join the waitlist.
  2. Use the subject “Remove my email” and tell us whether you want to stop waitlist emails, delete your waitlist entry, or both. If you cannot access that address, explain this so we can help verify your request.
  3. We handle requests manually and will confirm when the request has been completed. No account or sign-in is needed.
  4. To remove the copy stored on your device, open your browser’s settings and clear site data for appcaptures.com. Repeat for each browser or device you used. This clears local waitlist copies and cached pages, but does not remove the server entry; email us for that.

You do not need to install or open an app to make a removal request.
